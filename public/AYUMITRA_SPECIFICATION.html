AyuMitra Specification (PRD + TRD)

AI-Powered Clinical Intake Kiosk & Doctor Decision Support Workstation

1. Product Requirements (PRD) Overview

AyuMitra addresses outpatient congestion, doctor burnout, and linguistic barriers across Indian hospitals by introducing an automated, multilingual clinical pre-consultation workflow.

Key Capabilities

  • Multilingual Voice Case-Taking: Real-time speech conversation powered by OmniVoice and Groq LLM in Indian languages.
  • Direct Landing OCR Document Scanner: Automated transition to PaddleOCR for physical prescription and lab report digitization.
  • Emergency Red Flag Triage: Instant identification of cardiovascular, respiratory, and neurological critical symptoms.
  • Dual AYUSH & Modern Medicine Profiling: SNOMED CT / ICD-10 clinical coding + Prakriti, Agni, and Dosha mapping.
  • ABDM Compliance: Purpose-limited electronic consent under DPDP Act 2023 and immutable audit logging.

Target Personas

Persona Role Key Benefit
OPD Patient Rural / Low-literacy or Urban visitor Empathetic, voice-first self-intake in their mother tongue with zero typing.
Attending Doctor OPD Physician Pre-structured SOAP summary, instant red flag alerts, 60% consultation time saved.
Hospital Administrator CMO / Operations Head Reduced queue bottlenecks, verified ABDM/EHR sync, immutable audit trails.

4. Security & DPDP Compliance

  • Secret Isolation: API keys and connection strings stored strictly in .env.
  • SQL Injection Immunity: 100% parameterized queries via Prisma ORM.
  • Attack Mitigation: Helmet security headers, 120 req/min IP rate limiting, and 10MB payload cap.
  • Data Governance: Granular patient electronic consent and immutable audit logging (AuditLog).
