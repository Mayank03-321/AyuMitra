# Project: AyuMitra

## Executive Summary
AyuMitra is a comprehensive, intelligent clinical intake and clinical decision support system designed for Indian hospital OPDs. It bridges the gap between patient pre-consultation kiosks and physician dashboards with real-time AI triage, multilingual audio/text capture, prescription OCR, AYUSH integration, and ABDM compliance.

## Target Audience
1. **OPD Patients**: Rural and urban patients needing accessible (voice/multilingual/touch) kiosk intake and ABHA integration.
2. **Physicians & Doctors**: Hospital OPD doctors who need instant, structured, verified clinical summaries with red-flag detection and dual Allopathic/AYUSH insights.
3. **Hospital Administrators**: Requiring seamless ABDM compliance, audit trails, and HIS interoperability.

## Core Capabilities
* **Multilingual Patient Kiosk**: Touch + Speech input across Indian languages.
* **Smart Triaging & Red Flags**: Rule-based and LLM-powered emergency warning system.
* **Medical Document OCR**: Instant parsing of past prescriptions and lab reports.
* **Dual Modern & AYUSH Clinical Mapping**: SNOMED CT, ICD-10, and NAMASTE/AYUSH terminologies.
* **ABDM Consent & Security**: End-to-end patient consent tracking and immutable audit logging.
